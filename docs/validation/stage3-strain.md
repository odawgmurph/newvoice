# Stage 3 validation: the strain monitor

Generated by `harness/validation/strain.py --report` from `app/src/feedback/fixtures/strain-sessions.json` (core 0.0.1). Decision D18.

The monitor asks for a rest on any one of the handbook's warning signs (§2.4): jitter or shimmer rising over the session, CPPS falling, an utterance's median F0 above the comfortable p90, or effort ≥ 6/10. Trends compare the median of the last 3 scored utterances with the median of the session's first 3. Thresholds: jitter ×2 and +0.5 points; shimmer ×1.8 and +3 points; CPPS −5 dB.

## Limits

- The strained voices are synthetic: per-cycle jitter and shimmer and aspiration noise, not real hyperfunction or false-fold constriction. No recordings of strained trans voices are available (handbook §4.5), and asking someone to strain on purpose conflicts with the safety rules.
- Trend rules need six scored utterances (1 s voiced each), so the first minute of a session is covered only by the F0 and effort rules.
